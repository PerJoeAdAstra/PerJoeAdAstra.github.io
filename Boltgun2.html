Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2

Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 is a first-person shooter developed by Auroch Digital and published by Big Fan Games. It is set in the Warhammer 40,000 universe and is a direct sequel to Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun.

I worked as a generalist programmer working on a wide variety of systems across the project From production through to release. Notable inclusions are

  • Responsible for all of the UI programming including integrating common UI into our project, creating and managing a Code UI workflow, assisting the UI team to bring their visions to life
  • Updating AI behaviour adding additional abilities as well as maintaining and fixing overall AI functionality
  • Editor tooling, creating in world visualisers and validators to help catch issues before they became problems at runtime.
  • Updating and maintaining the existing Boltgun codebase
